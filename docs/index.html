Rules and decisions to make

To settle with the founders

Before S4 starts, on 18 January

  • Quotes. Does the « PROJET » slot, priced on quote, exist despite the "no quotes" promise?
  • Booking confirmation. Automatic once paid, or after the creative accepts? This decides when reviews open.
  • The 5% commission. Paid by the client, by the creative, or shared?
  • Intellectual property. The code belongs to the school; the transfer terms are still to be set.

How the team keeps to the schedule

  • Each owner builds the part of their domain and reviews the others' work in that domain: security, data, payments.
  • If a member falls behind, those who have finished their sprint deliverables help them first.
  • Design runs one sprint ahead of development: a screen's mockups are ready before it is built.
  • Sprint S8 freezes features: fixes only. It is the only slack in the schedule.
