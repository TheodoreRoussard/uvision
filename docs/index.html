The MVP, due on 14 February, also covers messaging between client and creative, and reviews once a booking is confirmed, across all of France and all twelve categories. Sprints S6 to S8 then build the rest of the specification: validation code, SMS, full verification, map, Graine de Champion, Uvision Award, company and school accounts, announcements (the client posts a need, creatives apply), AI recommendations and secondary features. Uvision Ads is set aside by the founders for a future version.

Rules and decisions to make

To settle with the founders

Before S3 starts, on 4 January

  • Quotes. Does the « PROJET » slot, priced on quote, exist despite the "no quotes" promise?
  • Booking confirmation. Automatic once paid, or after the creative accepts? This decides when reviews open.
  • Partner schools. Which schools, and which email domains prove a student's status (2% commission)?
  • « Experienced » filter, by 21 December. How many bookings completed on Uvision? The filter is empty at launch.
  • Intellectual property. The code belongs to the school; the transfer terms are still to be set.

Later, before the module that depends on it

  • Payment release, before S6 (15 February). Is the payment released when the validation code is entered, or when the photos are delivered?
  • Graine de Champion rules, before S7 (1 March). The role of speed in the score, the rewards, and how levels never reset while the score can go down.
  • Announcements, before S8 (15 March). Do creatives apply at their listed prices, or with a custom price close to a quote?

How the team keeps to the schedule

  • Each owner builds the part of their domain and reviews the others' work in that domain: security, data, payments.
  • If a member falls behind, those who have finished their sprint deliverables help them first.
  • Design runs one sprint ahead of development: a screen's mockups are ready before it is built.
  • After the MVP, modules are built in priority order: trust and session, community, companies and schools, then announcements, AI and secondary features.
  • Features freeze on 28 March, at the end of S8. S9 is for fixes and handover only: there is no other slack. Anything unfinished at the freeze is delivered specified and documented.
  • Nobody works on the project after 6 April: the founders launch it from the handover pack and its go-live procedure.
